Smart Chapters reads the spoken content of your active sequence and creates a structured list of YouTube chapters with clear titles and timestamps.

You can copy the list directly into a video description and optionally add the chapters as markers on the timeline.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the final sequence">
    Make the video you want to chapter active and check that its dialogue is clear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the chapter range">
    Choose the minimum and maximum number of chapters you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a brief (optional)">
    Mention important topics or ask for a particular structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate chapters">
    Click **Generate Chapters** and keep the editing application open while the sequence is processed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the result">
    Click **Copy text** for your video description, or **Add Markers** to place the chapter titles on the timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Min Chapters** | 1–30 | Minimum number of sections the tool should identify. |
| **Max Chapters** | up to 50 | Maximum number of sections it may return. |
| **Brief** | optional text | Gives context, names important topics, or requests a specific grouping. |

The first chapter begins at `00:00`, which is required by YouTube chapter formatting.

<Tip>
  Do not force too many chapters. A chapter should represent a real topic change, not every short pause or example.
</Tip>

## Writing a useful brief

- *“Separate the introduction, tutorial, pricing discussion, and conclusion.”*
- *“Keep every product demo as its own chapter.”*
- *“Use short, factual titles and keep the sponsor section separate.”*

The brief guides the organization. It does not replace the actual spoken content and cannot create chapters for topics that are not present.

## Review and export

Read the generated titles and check their timecodes before publishing. **Copy text** copies the complete chapter list in a format suitable for a description. **Add Markers** places each title at its timestamp on the active timeline.

<Note>
  Adding markers does not cut or rearrange the sequence. It only adds navigation points.
</Note>

## Common pitfalls

<Warning>
  - **Unclear or missing dialogue** produces weak chapter boundaries.
  - **A very high minimum** can split one topic into several artificial chapters.
  - **A very low maximum** can merge unrelated topics.
  - **Changing the sequence after generation** makes the timestamps outdated. Generate again after major edits.
  - **Editing during processing** can cause the analyzed audio to differ from the current timeline.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  For a 10 to 30 minute video, start with 5 minimum and 10 maximum chapters, then regenerate with a more specific brief only if the structure is too broad.
</Tip>
